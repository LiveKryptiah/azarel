ENGAGEMENT FAQS

Common questions regarding billing, scope handoffs, and payment methods.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept direct Philippine Bank Transfers (BDO, BPI, UnionBank), GCash, Maya, and international wire or credit card processing via Stripe for global teams.

Are there long-term contracts or lock-ins?

No. Monthly plans operate on a flexible month-to-month basis. You can pause or cancel anytime with zero cancellation penalties.

Do I own the source code and infrastructure?

Yes. All source code, Docker configs, and database schemas developed during our engagement belong 100% to you under your GitHub organization with permissive MIT/Proprietary licensing.

What happens if our needs don't fit these tiers?

We offer custom fixed-price milestone contracts tailored precisely to complex architectures. Use the "Start a Project" terminal to send your specifications.
